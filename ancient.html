Fixed to the screen, alive on scroll

The aura, starfield and grid stay pinned to the window while the page moves over them. The glyph columns, grid and drifting text shift at different speeds as you scroll, so the background feels deep instead of painted on.

The logo

The Quirky Quasar mark is redrawn as a hologram in the style of the city schematic from the original screensaver, and it flickers on the original's exact timeline: a fade, then scanning bands and three re-ignitions, a steady hold, and a short blackout. It turns slowly as it drifts around the screen, and every so often it glides back to the centre and rests there for fifteen seconds.
